Contract Penalties Analysis

Power BI | Wastewater Operations & Maintenance

Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes contract penalties associated with wastewater treatment plant operations and maintenance. It transforms penalty records into actionable insights to support financial monitoring, operational investigations, and improvement priorities.

Dashboard Preview

Key Performance Indicators

95,105.99 Total Penalties (SAR)
9 Penalty Cases
81.26% FOG Penalty Contribution

Analytical Approach

  • Penalty analysis by treatment plant
  • Pareto analysis by water quality parameter
  • Monthly penalty trend analysis
  • Detailed penalty records for investigation
  • Interactive filtering by year, month, and plant

Key Findings

Fats, Oils and Grease (FOG) accounted for 81.26% of the total penalty amount in the analyzed dataset.

This suggests that investigating FOG-related non-compliance should be a priority when developing corrective actions to reduce future penalties.

The dashboard also helps identify which treatment plants and reporting periods contribute most to financial losses.

Tools & Techniques

Power BI | Excel | Power Query | DAX | Data Modeling | KPI Development | Pareto Analysis

Business Value

The project demonstrates how combining operational expertise with data analytics can help transform contract penalty records into meaningful insights, supporting better decisions and continuous improvement in wastewater treatment operations.
