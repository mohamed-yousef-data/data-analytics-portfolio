Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to monitor the financial performance of a 36-month Operations & Maintenance contract.

The project combines contract management knowledge with data analytics to support informed financial decisions.

Business Problem

Managing a long-term O&M contract requires more than tracking monthly payments. Decision-makers need to understand spending patterns, contract utilization, penalties, and the relationship between elapsed time and financial progress.

Key Analytical Insights

Financial vs. Time Progress

Financial progress should be compared with elapsed contract time, but higher spending does not necessarily mean better operational performance.

Spare Parts Expenditure

Spare parts are purchased based on operational needs. Separating their costs from recurring O&M expenditure supports a more accurate financial assessment.

Penalties Monitoring

Monthly penalty trends highlight periods that require further investigation. Additional root-cause data would help identify preventive actions.

Monthly Financial Trends

Comparing monthly net statements helps identify unusual changes and investigate their financial drivers.

Tools & Technologies

Power BI | Excel | Power Query | DAX | Data Modeling

Business Value

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of contract financial performance, enabling more transparent monitoring, earlier identification of unusual trends, and better-informed management decisions.
