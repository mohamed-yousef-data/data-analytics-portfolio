## Featured Projects

### 1. O&M Contract Financial Dashboard
![O&M Contract Financial Dashboard](Screenshot%202026-10-10%2023-02-49.png)
### Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to monitor the financial performance of a 36-month Operations & Maintenance (O&M) contract.

The dashboard transforms monthly financial statements into actionable insights, helping management monitor contract utilization, financial progress, deductions, penalties, and spare parts expenditure.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Contract Value & Financial Progress
- Monthly Net Statements
- Time Progress vs. Financial Progress
- Deductions & Penalties
- Spare Parts Budget Utilization
- Remaining Contract Value

### Key Business Insights

**1. Financial vs. Time Progress**

Comparing financial progress with elapsed contract time helps identify differences between spending and project duration. However, higher financial progress does not necessarily indicate better operational performance.

**2. Spare Parts Expenditure**

Separating spare parts expenditure from recurring O&M costs provides a clearer understanding of financial performance.

**3. Penalties Analysis**

Tracking penalties over time helps identify unusual increases and supports further root cause analysis to reduce future financial losses.

**4. Monthly Financial Trends**

Analyzing monthly net statements helps identify spending patterns, unusual fluctuations, and periods requiring further investigation.
