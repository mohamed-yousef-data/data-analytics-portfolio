Project Overview

Interactive Power BI reporting across three water and wastewater treatment plants, combining operational monitoring, water quality analysis, daily performance trends, and environmental sustainability metrics.

Key Analysis Areas

  • Operations: treated water volume, energy consumption, energy intensity (kWh/m³), hydraulic loading, and sludge production.
  • Water Quality: COD and BOD removal efficiency, MLSS, effluent COD/BOD, turbidity, ammonia, residual chlorine, and non-compliance monitoring.
  • Daily Trends: plant-to-plant comparisons and identification of changes in production, energy use, and effluent quality.
  • Sustainability: tree counts, green area, estimated CO₂ sequestration, and air quality indicators.

Tools & Techniques

Power BI | Excel | Power Query | DAX | Data Modeling | KPI Development | Data Visualization | Performance Benchmarking

Figures shown are dashboard snapshots; operational changes require investigation before drawing causal conclusions. CO₂ sequestration is an estimate.

Key Insights & Findings

  • Treatment Efficiency: COD removal reached 96.83% and BOD removal reached 98.03% during the selected reporting period.
  • Energy Efficiency: Energy intensity (kWh/m³) should be evaluated alongside hydraulic loading and plant design capacity.
  • Operational Anomalies: Daily trends revealed unusual changes in energy consumption that require further operational investigation.
  • Water Quality Compliance: The dashboard reported zero non-compliant samples for the selected period and filters.
  • Environmental Sustainability: The dashboard tracks 3,769 trees, 13,650 m² of green area, and estimated annual CO₂ sequestration of 75.38 tonnes.

Business Value & Recommendations

Business Value

  • Operational Monitoring: Monitor treated water production, hydraulic loading, energy consumption, and sludge production across plants.
  • Energy Efficiency: Identify differences in kWh/m³ while considering actual flow and design capacity.
  • Water Quality: Evaluate COD, BOD, ammonia, and MLSS indicators to support compliance monitoring.
  • Early Detection: Identify unusual operational trends that may require investigation.

Recommendations

  • Investigate high energy intensity alongside hydraulic loading and equipment operating hours.
  • Review unexpected laboratory and operational readings to improve data quality.
  • Use normalized KPIs for fair plant comparisons.
  • Conduct regular KPI reviews to support maintenance and operational decisions.

Project Impact

This project demonstrates how combining wastewater treatment expertise with Power BI analytics supports data-driven decisions and identifies opportunities for operational improvement.

Operational Data Quality & Anomaly Detection

MLSS Trend Analysis | Power BI Case Study

An analysis of daily MLSS measurements across three wastewater treatment plants revealed unusual patterns that warrant further investigation.

Key Findings

  • February: Displayed relatively natural day-to-day fluctuations compared with the other months.
  • June, July & August: The blue series exhibited unusually smooth, repetitive wave-like patterns.
  • Additional observations: The green and orange series showed prolonged periods of limited variation.

Operational Value

Trend analysis can help identify potential data entry errors, repeated measurements, interpolation, and records requiring laboratory verification.

Key Takeaway: A KPI tells you what was reported. A trend can help you question whether the reported data is reliable.

Note: Plant identities are anonymized. Unusual patterns do not establish data manipulation without verification of original records.
